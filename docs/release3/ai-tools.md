## Use of ai-toos in the project

In our project, we don’t use AI tools to generate code directly, because it makes it harder to guarantee a certain level of quality. However, we do use AI tools to generate files that are less critical to the project, since AI is much faster than humans. For instance, we have used two types of AI to generate files; ChatGPT to create the .gitignore file, and Google Gemini to write the “commit-message.md”, “pr-checklist.md”, and a section of the README. In all files generated with AI, we have added comments explaining where and how we used it. When using AI, we are even more critical of its output compared to work done by a person. Indirectly, AI has also played an important role in our debugging process, as it is good at detecting small errors and bugs that we might not notice ourselves. For example, we have used AI to better understand issues related to Git. Even though AI can be good at spotting small errors, it’s usually not as strong at determining whether the code solves the task correctly or fits the overall purpose. Therefore, we have been extra careful when using AI for tasks that involve connecting different parts of the code. Nevertheless, we have used AI to generate drafts when writing the UI tests. This approach worked well because we already had ideas about how to implement them, and AI is very fast at producing drafts based on our concepts. To make this process safe, we followed guidelines outlining what to watch for and where AI tends to fall short when generating tests, for example, to avoid creating tests that simply confirm buggy behavior in the code. Apart from the UI tests, we have tried to avoid using AI in our code, mainly for learning purposes. You learn much more by dealing with problems as they arise and testing your way through them. In a nutshell, AI has been an additional resource that has assisted us throughout the process. We have been aware of its limitations and worked to isolate AI tools to tasks where they perform best. At the same time, we have avoided using AI in complex tasks that provide greater learning opportunities when solved manually.
