## Sustainability Reflection

To reflect on the sustainability aspects of our restaurant booking app, we used the Sustainability Awareness Framework (SusAF). The framework helps analyze the consequences of the system across five dimensions: individual, social, environmental, technical, and economic.

### Individual Dimension   

At the individual level, the app has several immediate positive effects. It simplifies restaurant reservations by collecting many restaurants in one platform, saving users time and effort. This convenience can also reduce mental load and stress, as all bookings are stored in one place. Users gain flexibility and control since they can easily modify or cancel bookings, which strengthens their sense of agency. However, there are immediate negative impacts regarding privacy and data security. User data is stored in an unprotected JSON file, creating risks for personal information. Over time, this could lead to mistrust and discourage use if not addressed. Structurally, it highlights the need for stronger digital responsibility and better data protection practices in similar apps.

### Social Dimension

Socially, the app opens up for more interaction by making it easier for people to go out and connect with others, supporting social inclusion and a sense of community. It also gives smaller restaurants visibility alongside larger ones, promoting equality within the restaurant industry. Although, structural risks exist if the platform allows businesses to pay for higher visibility, as this could favor wealthier restaurants and create inequality. On a broader level, the app could influence social behavior, encouraging people to engage digitally and make dining out more accessible.

### Environmental Dimension

From an environmental view, the app provides immediate benefits by combining multiple booking systems into one, reducing the need for several apps and slightly lowering energy use and data storage. Future versions could adapt more sustainable choices by showing which restaurants are environmentally friendly or use local ingredients. This would guide users toward greener decisions. Structurally, such features could influence longterm consumer habits and push the restaurant industry toward eco-friendly practices.

### Technical Dimension

Technically, the app is scalable and adaptable, allowing new features like menus, table orders, or payments to be added later. This creates strengthning effects by supporting continuous learning for developers and users. Structurally, it could inspire a broader move toward sentraled digital platform that forcus on sustainable design.
Economic Dimension
Economically, the app offers immediate benefits through time savings and simpler decision-making. Over time, it can help the growth in the restaurant sector by encouraging people to eat out more, increasing economic activity and potentially creating jobs. However, structural issues such as overconsumption or food waste may arise when more people are dining out frequenlty. On the positive side, the app can optimize operations through data analysis, reducing resource waste and supporting a more sustainable restaurant economy.

### Conclusion 

Using the Sustainability Awareness Framework, our booking app shows both positive and negative sustainability impacts. It provides immediate convenience, social inclusion, and technical scalability, but also raises concerns about data security and market inequality. Over time, it enables sustainable habits and efficient systems, and at a structural level, influence social norms and industry practices. By addressing these issues, the app can develop into a more comprehensive and sustainable solution.
