## Teamwork
We started to work on this assignment shortly after we submitted the first one, as we knew a lot of thing were missing. We then divided ourselves into three different pairs, each with our own primary responsibilities for tests, the UI, and the controller. In addition to this pair work, we had two weekly meetings, usually on Monday and Thursday, where we all met and worked together. This allowed us to help each other out, and it also shortened review times, enabling us to start working on other issues quickly. In addition to these meetings, we also had work sessions within our pairs, which allowed us to schedule work around our individual availability. During each meeting, we also discussed our progress and based on that we made decisions on what we should prioritize and who should be assigned the lesser tasks, such as writing the documentation. When we were almost done with our main responsibilities, we started reviewing the feedback from our study assistant. We then divided the smaller tasks individually so that we could work on more tasks simultaneously, such as creating user stories and adjusting structures. A few of us still met to handle some of the larger issues such as implementing Checkstyle and moving logic out of the controllers. Finally, we all met one last time to double-check the work we had done by systematically going through the assignment and previous feedback point by point to ensure everything was done.
