We started the work on this assignment. We then devided ourselves into three different pairs, each with our own main responsibilities for tests, ui, and controller modules. After this we had two weekly meetings, mainly on Monday and Thursday, when we all met and worked together. This allowed us to help eachother out, and it also made the reviewing time shorter, meaning we could start working on other issues fast. In addition to these meetings we also had worksessions in our pairs, which allowed us to find times that suited our sceduals better. In each meeting we also got an idea of how it was going with eachother and base don that we could make decitions on what we should prioritize and who can move on to doing other tasks, such as writing the documentation.
